Tasting Notes

A robust, multi-layered sweetness. Expect bright, herbaceous top notes giving way to a warm, toasted caramel finish. The texture is naturally thick and may crystallize over time.

Provenance & Harvest

Harvested seasonally from hives on our farmstead. Each batch is small, so flavour and colour change slightly each year.

The Farm to Table Process

We believe in minimal intervention. Our process respects the bees and preserves the natural integrity of the honey.

Untouched Forage

Our hives are situated miles away from industrial agriculture. The bees gather nectar exclusively from native wildflowers, ensuring a pure, pesticide-free product.

Living Superfood

We never heat or ultra-filter our honey. This preserves the natural pollen, propolis, and live enzymes.

  • Unpasteurized
  • Cold Extracted
  • Pesticide Free

Artisanal Extraction

Frames are removed by hand and spun using centrifugal force. The honey is strained only once through a coarse sieve, then hand-poured into glass jars.
